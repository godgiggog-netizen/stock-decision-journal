# Alert Outcome Review

Use for +1, +7, +30, and +90 checkpoint reviews.

At each checkpoint capture:
- price and performance if verified
- thesis status: strengthening, valid, weakening, or broken
- whether the original alert was early, timely, extended, crowded, or invalid
- catalyst progress and new risks
- whether entry quality differed from company quality
- the next review date or stop condition

At the final review, distinguish:
1. good company, good entry
2. good company, poor entry
3. poor company, lucky price move
4. poor company, poor entry

Never back-edit the original score to make the history look cleaner. Learning belongs in the review record.
