# Decision Journal Workflow

Use this skill for any change to thesis, position, review, or decision flows.

1. Capture what was known at the decision time.
2. Store the thesis as an append-only versioned record.
3. Record explicit break conditions and the expected review horizon.
4. Keep later evidence in a new thesis version or review record.
5. Separate business quality, valuation, entry quality, and portfolio fit.
6. When a decision changes, record the reason and evidence that caused the change.
7. Preserve old records so the user can compare original thinking with later outcomes.
8. Verify mobile readability and Thai wording before shipping.

Do not edit old thesis text merely because the later outcome is now known.
